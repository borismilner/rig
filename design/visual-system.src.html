01 The window

Achromatic shell, one hue per program

The shell has no colour of its own. Each program owns one hue from a uniform six-hue family, and while you are in that program it is the only saturated colour on screen. A host that wears the colour of whatever it is holding. The rail is a graduated collar, not a nav list: evenly spaced positions and one notch that slides. Click a program - the pane cross-fades through a view transition and the whole page re-hues with it, section numbers included.

Chrome budget. 56px rail + 36px context bar + 22px status strip. On 1400x900 that is 6.1%, a number that gets defended rather than a feeling. Everything else belongs to the program.

02 Status at rest

Healthy is the absence of colour

Six programs, most idle, most of the time. Anything that animates or glows at rest is a tax paid all day. So ok is drawn as nothing - a hollow tick - and a hue appears only when something wants you. A failing pip is the one thing on the surface allowed to move, and it breathes rather than blinks. This corrects the first palette note, which had sage standing for ok: a green dot on every row all day is exactly the noise this shell exists to remove.

03 The tray

Seven icons become one

Your panel as it is right now, and as it would be. Click the rig icon: the menu is a real popover, placed by CSS anchor positioning against the icon itself, so it cannot drift out of alignment the way a hand-placed one does.

Both strips keep your panel's real colour in either theme, because they are a picture of your panel rather than a surface of this design system. The rig icon is achromatic while everything is well, so a coloured tray icon is information rather than decoration.

04 Toasts

A designed surface, not a line handed to the desktop

Frameless, always-on-top, transparent webview windows rig owns and styles. Severity is the same six-hue family doing double duty, so nothing new is introduced anywhere in the product. Fire them: they spring in on a real linear() curve, the dwell line runs for about 10 s plus 5 s a line, hovering pauses it, and past the third they collapse into a deck that fans out on hover. Nothing is ever only a toast - everything lands in the centre with its action still live, which is what makes auto-dismiss safe.

Hover the deck to fan it. Hover a toast to hold it.

05 The palette

Generated, measured twice, and anchored to meaning

The family is produced in oklch at one lightness and one chroma, so "a family" is arithmetic rather than a promise: move the lightness and every member moves together. Then it is measured twice, against two different questions.

Can it be read?WCAG 2.x contrast, against every surface it can land on. 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for a component boundary. The table below, live. -worst ratio
Can two of them be told apart?CIEDE2000, the CIE's perceptual colour difference. ~1 is just noticeable, ~5 clearly different, 10+ unmistakable. A categorical set is only as good as its worst pair, never its average. -worst ΔE00, -

Red is bad and green is good everywhere a person has ever used a computer, so those angles are anchored and the optimiser may not move them. Amber is warn, blue is info. The free members are then placed to maximise that worst pair - press Optimise separation in the lab and watch the number climb without a single anchor shifting. And no program ever owns an alarm hue: a shell that turns red when you open a program is telling you something untrue, so a sixth program takes the achromatic accent, which the shell is anyway.

The neutrals are not chosen at all. --border, --fg-dim and --fg-faint are binary-searched to the dimmest value that still clears their WCAG target on every surface - which is how the inherited #556579 border, 2.40:1 on a panel, stops being something anyone can type.
